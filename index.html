How we calculated this

Daily menu items are synced automatically from Carolina Dining Services and mapped to their primary SIMAP Coolfood Pledge (2025) ingredient factor (derived from Poore & Nemecek, 2018).

  • Weekday vs. Weekend Periods: CDS serves Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner on weekdays, and Continental, Brunch, and Dinner on weekends.
  • Real-World Comparisons: Driving distance uses the U.S. EPA Greenhouse Gas Equivalencies passenger vehicle average of 0.39 kg CO₂e per mile. Smartphone charges use the EPA factor of 0.0124 kg CO₂e per full charge.
  • Portion Weights: Estimated using standard Carolina Dining Services serving portions converted to as-purchased ingredient weight.
